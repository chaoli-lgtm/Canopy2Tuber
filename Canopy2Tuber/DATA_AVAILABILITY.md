## Canopy2Tuber data availability statement

Canopy2Tuber provides the trained MST-Net weights, model configuration,
inference scripts, representative example spectra, and a biomass-prediction
demonstration for the integrated UAV phenotyping, PROSAIL-informed deep
learning, and dynamic crop-modeling workflow. These materials enable model
loading, example prediction, and verification of the principal computational
workflow.

The complete UAV imagery, complete field datasets, and all training code are
not publicly released because of data volume and their planned use in ongoing
projects. The complete data may be requested from the corresponding author for
reasonable scientific purposes, subject to institutional permission.
